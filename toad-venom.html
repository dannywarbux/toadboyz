Toad Venom Strain Guide

What is Toad Venom?

Toad Venom is a high-potency indica-dominant hybrid cannabis strain and the flagship flower of the Frögy brand by Toadboyz. Grown in small batches in Los Angeles, California, Toad Venom tests at 28.4% THC and delivers a heavy, full-body experience that creeps slow and hits hard. It's the strain that put Frögy on the map — the toad everyone's chasing, sealed in a matte-black collectible jar.

Toad Venom Effects

Toad Venom hits with a creeping body-melt that starts behind the eyes and rolls down through the shoulders and chest. At moderate doses it's social and euphoric — a warm, heavy buzz that doesn't lock you to the couch. At higher doses, it's a full-body sedative built for the end of the night. Expect deep physical relaxation, slow time perception, and a heavy-lidded calm. This is not a morning strain.

Toad Venom THC Percentage

Frögy Toad Venom tests at 28.4% THC — well above the California legal-market median of 18-23%. Every batch is lab-tested in California and sold in 3.5g (1/8 oz) matte-black collectible jars. THC results are confirmed on Certificate of Analysis (COA) before any drop ships.
