The Story

Frögy started with one dot. The third eye — the dot you watch.

We're a standalone Los Angeles cannabis house. No parent brand, no borrowed name, no co-sign. Just Frögy and the Toadboyz — a small crew obsessed with loud color, matte-black packaging, and flower that actually earns the hype.

Every batch is grown small, hand-selected, and sealed the same day. One flagship flower, two pre-roll blends, one frog. What you see on the shelf is what's in the jar — tested, stamped, and dated.

We don't chase trends. We build drops. The frog watches. You listen.
